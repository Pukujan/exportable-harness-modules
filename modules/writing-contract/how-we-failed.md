# How we failed (writing)

These claims were tried in the Kilo colorful-income session and later killed. They remain queryable as superseded evidence.

| Killed claim | Why it failed | Current property |
|---|---|---|
| Always open with `## TL;DR` | Tiny answers looked like documents. Users could not skim a short result. | **W2** TL;DR only on long writeups |
| Crush replies to 1–4 dense lines | Kilo’s default “be terse” made chat unreadable. | **W3** real paragraphs |
| Break every 2–3 lines | Telegram rhythm, not briefing rhythm | **W3** paragraphs may run several sentences |
| Lead with px / jsonc / selectors | Users asked how it *felt*, not how the CSS was patched | **W4** notice first |

Do not promote chat claims to current-best without `checkers/writing.py`.
